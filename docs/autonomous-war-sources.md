# Autonomous War — research notes and sources

`AutonomousWar` is an original strategic explainer, not operational guidance.
It distinguishes remote-controlled drones from partial and higher autonomy,
emphasizes human judgment and accountability, and focuses on force design.

## Core findings used in the film

- Autonomy is a spectrum. Current systems commonly automate navigation,
  stabilization, sensing, and decision support while human oversight remains
  important, especially for engagement decisions.
- Recent wars demonstrate the value of small, attritable systems, rapid
  iteration, and uncrewed platforms across air, land, and sea.
- Electronic warfare makes resilient navigation, edge processing, and graceful
  failure essential.
- The strategic unit is the connected sensing-to-decision system, not an
  isolated drone.
- India has active AI, innovation, indigenous UAV, and high-altitude testing
  programs, but needs scalable software, testing, production, counter-UAS, and
  governance ecosystems.
